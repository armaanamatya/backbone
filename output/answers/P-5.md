## 7. Assumptions

The figures cover the documented record. A date with no document is counted as a date with no contact, and is named where it falls in the period.
A week that runs past the end of the episode is judged against the full requirement and labelled partial.
A verdict is "met" only if every alternative meets the requirement, "not met" only if none does, and "cannot be determined" otherwise.
The reason a week fell short, such as a cancellation, is context and does not change the verdict.
A session by video counts as patient-present.
A session the patient attended in part counts as a session and a therapy day; only its minutes are reduced.
An interval a document says had no therapy, such as a group break or a lost connection, is removed from the minutes.
"Therapy" means the classes the plan BH-D003 counts: family therapy, group therapy, individual therapy, with the patient present.
